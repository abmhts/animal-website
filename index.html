Black Tiger

Black tigers are rare pseudo-melanistic Bengal tigers with unusually broad, merged black stripes that make their orange coat look mostly dark.

Key Facts About Black Tigers

  • Not Truly Black: They are not completely black; vibrant orange fur still shines through wide, dense stripes.
  • Only Place on Earth: Wild populations live exclusively in the Similipal Tiger Reserve located in Odisha, India.
  • Extremely Rare: Scientists believe only about 10 of these unique big cats remain in the wild.
